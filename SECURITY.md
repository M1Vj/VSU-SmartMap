# Security policy

## Supported version

Security fixes target the latest release and the `main` branch.

| Version | Supported |
| --- | --- |
| Latest `main` deployment | Yes |
| Older commits and deployments | No |

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting page for this repository:
`https://github.com/M1Vj/VSU-SmartMap/security/advisories/new`.

Do not open a public issue containing exploit steps, credentials, personal
information, private storage objects, or sensitive campus details. Include the
affected route/component, reproduction steps, impact, and a suggested fix when
available. The maintainer will acknowledge a complete report, assess severity,
and coordinate a fix and disclosure timeline.

Expected response targets are acknowledgement within 3 business days, an
initial severity assessment within 7 business days, and status updates at
least every 14 days until resolution. Timelines may be shortened for actively
exploited or critical vulnerabilities.

## Safe harbor

Good-faith research that follows this policy, avoids privacy violations and
service disruption, and stops after demonstrating impact will not be pursued
by the project. Do not retain, alter, or publish data belonging to another
person. Report accidental access immediately and delete any captured data.

Never test against accounts, data, or infrastructure you do not own or have
permission to assess. Automated destructive testing and access to private user
uploads are out of scope.
