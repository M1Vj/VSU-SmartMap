# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give actionable feedback, accept correction, and prioritize the
well-being and privacy of the campus community. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and other
conduct reasonably considered inappropriate are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub
profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be fair,
proportionate, and respectful of reporter confidentiality.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
