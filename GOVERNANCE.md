# Governance

Campus SmartMap is a maintainer-led open-source project. The primary maintainer
sets release scope, reviews changes, manages security disclosures, and protects
the accuracy and safety of campus data.

Decisions are made in public issues and pull requests when possible. Changes
are evaluated for user benefit, evidence quality, accessibility, privacy,
security, maintainability, and compatibility with the unofficial student-led
mission. Significant architectural or policy changes should include a written
rationale and migration plan.

Consistent contributors may receive triage or review responsibility after a
record of constructive, secure work. Repository administration and release
authority remain with the maintainer until explicitly delegated. Conflicts of
interest should be disclosed, and university affiliation must never be implied
without authorization.
